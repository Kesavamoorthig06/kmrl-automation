# KMRL Login QR Codes (working set)

These QR codes encode the exact role keys used by the app login. Scanning one logs in and redirects to the correct dashboard.

| Role              | QR Data   | File                                      | Redirects to      |
|-------------------|-----------|-------------------------------------------|-------------------|
| Administrator     | `admin`   | KMRL_Administrator_admin.png             | /dashboard        |
| Branding Officer  | `brand`   | KMRL_Branding_Officer_brand.png          | /branding_officer |
| Cleaning Crew     | `clean`   | KMRL_Cleaning_Crew_clean.png              | /cleaning         |
| Operation Staff   | `operation` | KMRL_Operation_Staff_operation.png      | /operation_staff  |
| Technical Staff   | `tech`    | KMRL_Technical_Staff_tech.png             | /technical        |
| Yard Operations   | `yard`    | KMRL_Yard_Operations_yard.png            | /yard             |

**Use:** On the login page, click “Open Camera” and scan the QR for the role. The app redirects automatically.

**Note:** For production, use secure credentials; these are for testing only.
