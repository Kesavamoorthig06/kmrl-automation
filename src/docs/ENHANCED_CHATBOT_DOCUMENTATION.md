# Enhanced KMRC Chatbot Documentation

## Overview
The KMRC (Kochi Metro Rail Corporation) chatbot has been significantly enhanced with comprehensive knowledge about metro operations, cultural events, festivals, and local context. It now functions as an advanced AI assistant with LLM-like capabilities, providing intelligent, context-aware responses.

## Cultural Events & Festivals

### Major Festivals

#### 1. Onam (August-September)
- **Malayalam**: ഓണം | **Hindi**: ओणम
- **Duration**: 10 days (Atham to Thiruvonam)
- **Description**: Kerala's biggest harvest festival celebrating King Mahabali's homecoming
- **Metro Impact**: High ridership, special services may be announced
- **Special Services**: Extended hours during Thiruvonam day

#### 2. Vishu (April)
- **Malayalam**: വിഷു | **Hindi**: विषु
- **Duration**: 1 day
- **Description**: Kerala's New Year celebration with Vishukkani and traditional rituals
- **Metro Impact**: Moderate increase, especially morning temple visits
- **Special Services**: Normal operations with crowd management

#### 3. Christmas (December)
- **Malayalam**: ക്രിസ്മസ് | **Hindi**: क्रिसमस
- **Duration**: 1 day
- **Description**: Celebrated with great enthusiasm, especially in Fort Kochi
- **Metro Impact**: High ridership to Fort Kochi area
- **Special Services**: Extended services to Fort Kochi

#### 4. New Year (December 31 - January 1)
- **Malayalam**: പുതുവത്സരം | **Hindi**: नया साल
- **Duration**: 2 days
- **Description**: New Year celebrations with parties and fireworks
- **Metro Impact**: Very high ridership on December 31st night
- **Special Services**: Extended late-night services, additional security

#### 5. Thrissur Pooram (April-May)
- **Malayalam**: തൃശൂർ പൂരം | **Hindi**: त्रिशूर पूरम
- **Duration**: 36 hours
- **Description**: One of Kerala's most spectacular temple festivals
- **Metro Impact**: High ridership from Kochi to Thrissur
- **Special Services**: Special arrangements for intercity travelers

### Cultural Events

#### 1. Kochi-Muziris Biennale (December-March)
- **Duration**: 3 months (every two years)
- **Description**: International contemporary art exhibition
- **Metro Impact**: Significant increase in international tourists
- **Special Services**: Tourist-friendly announcements

#### 2. Kochi Carnival (December)
- **Duration**: 10 days
- **Description**: Annual carnival with parades and cultural shows
- **Metro Impact**: High ridership to Fort Kochi and Marine Drive
- **Special Services**: Extended services to Fort Kochi

#### 3. Kerala Tourism Week (October)
- **Duration**: 7 days
- **Description**: Week-long celebration promoting Kerala tourism
- **Metro Impact**: Increased tourist footfall to heritage sites
- **Special Services**: Tourist information services
